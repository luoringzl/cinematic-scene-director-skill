# Stylized Art Direction, Scene States, Functional Shots & VFX Database

This database supplements the 12 Realist Styles (`SKILL.md`) with the **Intelligent Style Advisor Decision Matrix**, **6 High-Concept Stylized Art Directions (Styles 13–18)**, **5 Scene State Modifiers**, **Functional Shot Grammar**, and **Practical-Feel VFX Overlays**.

## Part I: Intelligent Style Advisor Decision Matrix (智能美术风格诊断与推荐树)

When a user provides a script, synopsis, or genre without knowing what visual style fits best, diagnose along **3 Axes (Era/Setting × Core Emotional Tone × Realism vs. Stylized Ratio)** and proactively recommend **2 to 3 distinct visual options**:

1. **Option 1: Grounded Historical/Genre Realism (正统写实派)** — Best for strong narrative immersion and physical credibility (pull from Styles 01–12).
2. **Option 2: High-Concept Stylized Art Direction (高概念风格化派)** — Best for striking visual hooks, auteur aesthetics, and mythic/psychological impact (pull from Stylized 13–18 below).
3. **Option 3: Hybrid Blend (跨流派融合派)** — Combine 70% realist base with 30% stylized geometry/color/VFX (e.g., *Style 01 Ink-Cyan Realism* + *Stylized 14 Villeneuve Monolithic Scale*).

## Part III: 5 Narrative Scene State Modifiers (同一场景·5大叙事状态转换词库)

1. **State 01 | Normal Lived-in State (日常完好态)**: `in pristine lived-in daily condition, neatly arranged period props, calm ambient lighting`
2. **State 02 | Post-Battle Damaged State (激战损毁态)**: `in chaotic post-battle aftermath, shattered wooden furniture, deep blade slashes or bullet impacts across pillars and walls, overturned tables, drifting smoke and glowing embers on the completely dry floor`
3. **State 03 | Decades-Abandoned State (百年荒废态)**: `decades-abandoned and reclaimed by nature, thick layers of undisturbed grey dust and cobwebs covering all furniture, partially collapsed roof letting in shafts of cold light, dead vines and dry leaves scattered across the dry floor`
4. **State 04 | Blackout Stealth State (断电潜入/夜袭态)**: `in complete power-blackout stealth state, all indoor practical lamps extinguished, illuminated solely by faint cold moonlight or street glare slanting through windows, long ominous shadows`
5. **State 05 | Supernatural / Array Active State (超自然异象/阵法激活态)**: `in supernatural ritual activation state, subtle anti-gravity dust and stone pebbles floating inches above the floor, faint glowing geometric runes or dark wraith mist pulsing along architectural seams, unstable flickering light`

## Part IV: Functional Shot Grammar & Practical-Feel VFX Overlays

- **90-Degree Top-Down God's Eye View**: `strict 90-degree overhead bird's-eye top-down planimetric view looking straight down at the floor layout`
- **Macro Prop Insert Shot**: `extreme close-up macro insert shot with razor-thin shallow depth of field (85mm f/1.4 macro lens), creamy background bokeh`
- **Doorway / Corridor Threshold POV Push-In**: `first-person threshold POV shot framed symmetrically through a half-open doorway looking straight into the room depth`
- **Dutch Angle Psychological Tilt**: `tense 15-degree Dutch canted angle framing creating psychological unease and dynamic diagonal tension`
