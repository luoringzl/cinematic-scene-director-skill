# Cinematography Lighting & Optical Database (DP Reference)

Always construct lighting using the **4-Part DP Formula**: `[Light Source Motivation] + [Geometry/Gobo Quality] + [Kelvin Color Temp Contrast] + [Atmospheric Media]`.

## 1. Chiaroscuro & Geometric Gobo Lighting (几何切割与光影遮挡)

- **Venetian Blind Shadow Stripes (百叶窗条状硬光切割)**:
  - `harsh cold sidelight filtering through dusty aluminum Venetian blinds, casting razor-sharp chiaroscuro shadow stripes (slats) across the desk and walls`
- **Curtain Gap Razor Beam (窗帘一线窄缝刀锋光)**:
  - `heavy blackout curtains drawn shut leaving only a narrow 10cm vertical gap, a single razor-sharp beam of city neon light slicing through the darkness across the completely dry floor`
- **Under-Door Light Spill (门缝底部漏光)**:
  - `ominous under-door light spill, a thin horizontal wedge of harsh warm corridor light cutting across the pitch-black room floor from beneath a closed wooden door`
- **Chinese Lattice Window Projections (中式雕花窗棂/窗纸光效)**:
  - *Daylight Lattice*: `angled afternoon sunlight passing through intricate Chinese wooden lattice windows, projecting crisp geometric light-and-shadow patterns onto the dry square-tile floor`
  - *Moonlight Mulberry Paper*: `soft diffused cold cyan moonlight filtering through translucent aged mulberry window paper, casting faint bamboo-leaf silhouettes`

## 2. Practical Lights & Localized Pools (室内实景道具光源)

- **Localized Desk Lamp Pool (局部暖池台灯+四周死黑衰减)**:
  - `localized pool of warm emerald-and-amber light from a vintage green glass banker's lamp on the central desk, rapid light falloff plunging the surrounding towering bookshelves into deep velvety shadow`
- **Flickering Stairwell & Corridor Overhead Lights (声控灯与老旧日光灯频闪)**:
  - *Motion-Sensor Bulb*: `a single flickering overhead circular motion-sensor ceiling light on the mid-landing, casting high-contrast top-down chiaroscuro shadows while upper and lower stair flights vanish into complete pitch-black darkness`
  - *Aged Fluorescent Tube*: `harsh overhead aged fluorescent tube light with blackened ends, emitting a sickly cyan-green flickering glare with hard shadows`
- **Candlelight, Oil Lamps & Hearth Embers (古典烛火与壁炉余烬)**:
  - *Chinese Oil Lamp (2000K)*: `a single flickering warm amber flame (2000K) from a bronze oil lamp, creating soft warm falloff on nearby wood patina while leaving distant corners in deep ink-cyan shadow`
  - *Stone Fireplace Embers*: `low-angle glowing orange embers inside a cavernous stone fireplace, casting long dancing dramatic shadows across the rough flagstone floor`

## 3. Natural & Extreme Weather Lighting (户外自然天光与极端气象光效)

- **Volumetric God Rays (破云丁达尔神圣光束)**:
  - `dramatic volumetric golden god rays (crepuscular rays) breaking through heavy dark storm clouds, illuminating specific mountain tiers and suspended mist particles`
- **Stormy Night Bi-Color Contrast (暴风雨夜冷暖双色温极限反差)**:
  - `heavily underexposed stormy night sky (6500K cold slate-blue ambient top light) clashing with faint diffused warm orange cannon embers (2200K) on the distant horizon, side-backlit rain streaks`
- **Blue Hour & Golden Hour Backlight (蓝调时刻与黄昏逆光)**:
  - *Blue Hour*: `post-sunset twilight blue hour ambient light, deep cobalt sky gradient meeting early glowing city streetlights below`
  - *Golden Hour Backlight*: `low-angle grazing golden hour sunset backlight (2800K), creating glowing rim-light halos on dust particles and stretching long shadows across the ground`

## 4. Urban Night, Streetlamps & Neon Optics (城市夜景与霓虹光学反射)

- **Single Streetlamp Alley Axis (孤立路灯“一明一暗”对峙轴线)**:
  - `stark single-source overhead municipal LED streetlamp lighting, dividing the narrow alley into a bright cold-lit foreground zone and an impenetrable pitch-black shadow zone in the background, wet puddles providing specular bounce light`
- **Rain-Streaked Window Neon Bokeh (雨窗焦外霓虹散景渗透)**:
  - `out-of-focus vibrant crimson and cyan street neon bokeh bleeding through rain-streaked window glass, casting soft distorted color washes into the unlit room`

## 5. Atmospheric Media & Volumetric Depth (大气介质与体积散射)

- **Indoor Suspended Dust Motes (干燥室内光柱浮尘)**:
  - `dry microscopic dust motes lazily suspended and sparkling inside a sharp diagonal shaft of light`
- **Outdoor Mist & Cloud Separation (室外雨雾与贴地流云)**:
  - *Night Rain Halation*: `heavy night mist and fine rain spray diffusing bright streetlights into soft glowing halos (halation)`
  - *Volumetric Cloud Valley*: `dense volumetric white clouds and low-lying ground fog rolling between architectural foundations, creating natural atmospheric separation between foreground and background`

## 6. Camera, Lenses & Film Stock Locks (摄影机、镜头畸变与胶片底座)

- **Anamorphic Lenses (变形宽银幕镜头)**:
  - `shot on Arri Alexa LF with Cooke Anamorphic /i SF lenses, subtle horizontal blue/amber lens flare streaks on point light sources, oval-shaped bokeh in the background`
- **70mm Large Format Spherical (70毫米大画幅球面定焦镜头)**:
  - `shot on Arri Alexa 65 with Panavision Primo 70 spherical lenses, expansive field of view, natural depth of field falloff, razor-sharp micro-contrast`
- **35mm Film Halation & Crushed Blacks (胶片光晕与死黑暗部)**:
  - `Kodak Vision3 500T 35mm film stock, subtle warm halation around bright practical light edges, deep crushed black shadows with preserved mid-tone texture, authentic organic film grain`
