## Part II: Chinese Dynasties, Pagodas, Xianxia & Modern Asian Cities (中国历代、古塔、仙侠与近现代场景)

- **Pre-Qin / Han / Tang Imperial & Pagodas (先秦秦汉盛唐与古塔)**: Rammed earth high terraces (夯土高台), monumental black-and-red lacquered dougong brackets, or octagonal white Fangshan jade-marble pagodas with strictly straight horizontal non-upturned eaves, cast-bronze wind-bells under every corner, and Seal Script (篆书) doorway plaques.
- **Song / Ming / Qing Classical Courtyard & Study (宋明清庭院与雅室)**: Taihu scholar rocks, rain-drenched bamboo verandas, Jinzhuan dry square floor tiles, ironwood painting tables, celadon brush washers, and mulberry paper lattice windows.
- **Xianxia / Oriental High-Fantasy Cliff City (仙侠奇观主城池)**: Translucent malachite crystal peaks with subsurface scattering, hexagonal columnar black obsidian cliffs with raw gold ore veins, cantilevered carbonized golden-thread nanmu pavilions suspended over volumetric cloud valleys, and iridescent star-sand rivers.
- **1930s Republican Shanghai Shikumen (民国上海石库门)**: Weathered grey-and-red brick Shikumen alleyways, carved cement archways, black lacquered timber doors with brass knockers, wet granite cobblestone (Dangelu) streets.
- **1990s Southern Chinese Tenement (90年代南方老式筒子楼)**: Open-air atrium corridors, cracked concrete parapets revealing red brick, monsoon water-stained plaster walls, yellowed surface-mounted PVC conduits, flickering cyan-white fluorescent tubes.
